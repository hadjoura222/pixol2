About Unix Timestamps

          

            A Unix timestamp (also called Epoch time or POSIX time) is the number of seconds that have
            elapsed since January 1, 1970, at 00:00:00 UTC. It is a compact, timezone-independent way
            to represent a point in time, making it the standard for databases, APIs, and log files.
          

          

            This tool accepts timestamps in seconds or milliseconds and converts them to multiple
            human-readable formats, including ISO 8601, UTC, local time, and relative ("3 days ago").
